## ⭐ Our picks
- ⭐ **Green Lake at golden hour** — the Siberian gulls are the whole reason this night is more than a hotel. Fifteen minutes from the centre, free, and loud
- ⭐ **Wenlin Street and the university quarter for dinner** — Yunnan coffee, crossing-the-bridge noodles, and no tourist mark-up
- ⭐ **Pack before you go out** — this night exists to make the Dec 21 exit unmissable. Bag closed, alarm set, then enjoy the evening
- ⭐ **Check in online and screenshot the boarding pass** — the mainland exit is the one flight of the trip with no margin behind it
- ⭐ **Yuantong Temple**, five minutes from Green Lake — Kunming's oldest, built down into a hollow so the halls sit at water level

## ➕ More options
- **Crossing-the-bridge noodles done properly** — the broth arrives boiling under its oil lid and you cook the plates into it in order. Ask, then follow the table next to you
- **Guandu Ancient Town** — a restored temple quarter with real snack streets, out on the line towards the airport
- **Yunnan Provincial Museum** — big, modern and free *(verify opening days and whether booking is needed)*
- **The Dounan flower market** — the largest in Asia and it trades late into the night. A genuinely strange hour *(verify the trading times)*
- **Nanping Street and Zhengyi Road** for the evening crowds if the lake is too cold to linger
- **The metro to Changshui airport** is cheap and reliable — but check the first train of the morning against your flight *(verify the timetable)*
- Do **not** plan the Stone Forest. It is ninety minutes each way and this is not the day to be far from the airport
- Rest option: a Yunnan coffee house and nothing else. The trip has been long and tomorrow is a border

## 🍜 Where to eat
- ⭐ **桥香园 Qiao Xiang Yuan** (founded 1988) or **建新园 Jianxin Yuan** (a century old, and there is always a queue) — the two names Kunming itself gives for **过桥米线 crossing-the-bridge rice noodles**
- ⭐ **云南过桥米线**, 66 Cuihu South Road — the closest good bowl to Green Lake, with a view of it
- **江氏兄弟桥香园 Brothers Jiang**, Xiaoximen, by Green Lake — the big reliable chain; authentic and everywhere
- **The broth arrives boiling under an oil lid and you cook the plates into it in order.** Ask, then copy the next table
- ⚠️ **Time this against the train, not the appetite.** The plan arrives at **Kunming Station 昆明站**, central, which is what makes Green Lake possible; the fallback is **Kunming South**, then straight out. The bed is at the **airport** either way — the ibis's free transfer is the airport shuttle, **not** a station or city pickup (taxi / DiDi transfer time *verify*)
- ⚠️ **Nothing in the city on the morning of Dec 21.** That is the visa exit and the plan keeps it empty on purpose
