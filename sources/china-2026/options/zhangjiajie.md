## ⭐ Our picks
- ⭐ **Yuanjiajie on the clearest morning** — the Hallelujah pillar and the First Bridge are the view you came for. Take the Bailong lift up and walk down
- ⭐ **Tianzi Mountain late in the afternoon** — the pillar sea with low sun, and on a good day the fog sits *below* you rather than over you
- ⭐ **Yangjiajie and Wulong Zhai** — the quietest of the four areas and the best ridge walking. In December you can have it nearly to yourself
- ⭐ **Treat the multi-day park ticket as three flexible days**, not a schedule. The weather decides the order, you decide nothing *(verify the current validity and the passport registration at the gate)*
- ⭐ **Save Tianmen Mountain for your best forecast day** — the glass skywalk and the 99 bends are worth waiting for, and the cableway closes in ice

## ➕ More options
- **Golden Whip Stream** — the flat 7 km walk along the valley floor between the pillars. The right call when the tops are in cloud
- **Shili Gallery monorail** in Suoxi Valley — ten minutes under the "ten-mile" pillar wall, almost no effort
- **The canyon boardwalk below the glass bridge** — the bridge is the headline, the walk down through the gorge is the better hour
- **A Tujia song-and-dance evening** in Wulingyuan — the local folk show *(verify it is running in December, some pause off-season)*
- **Zhangjiajie city for a rest afternoon** — Puguang Temple and the riverside are ten minutes from the Tianmen cableway base
- **Xibu Street** in Wulingyuan for late food; the town otherwise empties early in winter
- Fog day, no view at all: **Huanglong Cave** is entirely underground, so the weather stops mattering
- Practicalities: stone stairs stay slick under fog — grip beats warmth in the shoes. Keep food zipped away from the monkeys near the stream, and keep a little cash for the small vendors *(verify locally)*
