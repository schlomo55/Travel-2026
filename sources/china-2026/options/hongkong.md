## ⭐ Our picks
- ⭐ **Symphony of Lights at 20:00 from the TST promenade** — free, five minutes from the hotel, and best taken on the first clear night rather than saved
- ⭐ **Man Mo Temple, Hollywood Road and the Mid-Levels escalator** — one continuous walk through the oldest part of the island, incense to antiques to bars
- ⭐ **Tai Kwun**, the old Central Police Station — free galleries and courtyards in a restored Victorian compound. The best rainy-day building in Hong Kong
- ⭐ **A ferry to Lamma or Cheung Chau** — an hour out of the city for the price of a coffee, and the island villages are the antidote to Christmas crowds
- ⭐ **Book the Christmas meal now, whatever it is** — Dec 24–26 the city is full and walk-ins get what is left *(verify: Dec 25 and 26 are public holidays and many places run holiday menus only)*

## ➕ More options
- **Big Buddha and Po Lin Monastery** on Lantau — the cable car if the day is clear, the bus if it is not *(verify the cable car is running; it closes for maintenance periods)*
- **Sham Shui Po** — fabric, electronics and the best cheap food in the city. Kowloon with none of the polish
- **Wong Tai Sin Temple** — fortune sticks and incense smoke, one stop from Chi Lin on the same line
- **M+ and the West Kowloon waterfront** — the big modern museum, walkable from TST along the harbour *(verify ticketing)*
- **PMQ and the Sheung Wan galleries** — design studios in a former police quarters, an easy hour off Hollywood Road
- **Tai O stilt village** on Lantau — fishing houses on poles over the water, best combined with the Big Buddha
- **Macau by ferry** — an hour each way and a separate border crossing *(verify entry rules and that it fits the visa plan before booking)*
- **The Star Ferry after dark, both directions** — a few dollars, ten minutes, and still the best view in the city
