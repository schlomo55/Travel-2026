## ⭐ Our picks
- ⭐ **The "Immersive Light Show in Central"** — WinterFest's headline — and the Christmas Eve answer whether or not Symphony of Lights is still running. **Free**, nightly, light *and* music: 4K 3D projection mapped onto **eight landmark buildings** (Bank of China Building and Tower, the Court of Final Appeal, City Hall High Block, the Hong Kong Club, HSBC Main, Prince's Building, Standard Chartered), a dozen-plus scenes, trees and footbridges lit with it, **open to 1 am over Christmas**. Reach it by **Star Ferry** — the crossing is half the pleasure *(verify this season's dates; WinterFest is re-announced each November)*
- ⭐ **The TST promenade after dark** — free, five minutes from the hotel, and the skyline is the show. ℹ️ **A Symphony of Lights: retirement announced (2026–27 budget), date not set** — it was still running on 21 Sep 2026. Ask on arrival; if it is on, the 20:00 show is a free bonus, and the view stands on its own either way
- ⭐ **Man Mo Temple, Hollywood Road and the Mid-Levels escalator** — one continuous walk through the oldest part of the island, incense to antiques to bars
- ⭐ **Tai Kwun**, the old Central Police Station — free galleries and courtyards in a restored Victorian compound. The best rainy-day building in Hong Kong
- ⭐ **A ferry to Lamma or Cheung Chau** — an hour out of the city for the price of a coffee, and the island villages are the antidote to Christmas crowds
- ⭐ **Book the Christmas meal now, whatever it is** — Dec 24–26 the city is full and walk-ins get what is left *(verify: Dec 25 and 26 are public holidays and many places run holiday menus only)*

## ➕ More options
- **Big Buddha and Po Lin Monastery** on Lantau — the Dec 25 plan: early dim sum, **at the Ngong Ping 360 cable car by ~09:30**, Tai O in the afternoon; the bus if the cable car is not running *(verify; it closes for maintenance periods)*
- **Sham Shui Po** — fabric, electronics and the best cheap food in the city. Kowloon with none of the polish
- **Wong Tai Sin Temple** — fortune sticks and incense smoke, one stop from Chi Lin on the same line
- **M+ and the West Kowloon waterfront** — the big modern museum, walkable from TST along the harbour *(verify ticketing)*
- **PMQ and the Sheung Wan galleries** — design studios in a former police quarters, an easy hour off Hollywood Road
- **Tai O stilt village** on Lantau — fishing houses on poles over the water, best combined with the Big Buddha
- **Macau by ferry** — an hour each way and a separate border crossing *(verify entry rules and that it fits the visa plan before booking)*
- **The Star Ferry after dark, both directions** — a few dollars, ten minutes, and still the best view in the city
- **AIA Carnival, Central Harbourfront** — opens for the season around **22 Dec** and runs to March: a ferris wheel over the harbour, rides, games and food stalls, five minutes from the Central light show *(verify this year's dates and admission)*
- **Aqua Luna**, the red-sail junk — 45 minutes on Victoria Harbour with a drink included, bookable on Klook. The paid way to see the harbour lit, and it needs no show to be running
- **Drone shows over Victoria Harbour** — Hong Kong flew several through 2026, including 2,026 drones for the handover anniversary. Nothing is scheduled at Christmas, but it is the format the city is moving to — worth asking *(verify)*
- 🎻 **HK Phil, "Festive Cadenza: Once Upon a Christmas Dream"** — **22 & 23 Dec, 19:30**, Cultural Centre Concert Hall, **HK$260–680**, ~2h15. Joyce Cheung (piano), Lio Kuokman conducting, the Hong Kong Children's Choir. The hall is **1 km from the bed**, straight back along the waterfront. ⏰ **Tickets open on URBTIX at 10:00 on 6 Aug** *(verify the repertoire — it is billed as holiday classics, and the full programme is not published)*
- 🩰 **Hong Kong Ballet, "The Nutcracker: A Hong Kong Adventure"** — **11–27 Dec**, Grand Theatre of the same building, with the **Hong Kong Sinfonietta live** under Matthew Rowe. **Live music HK$260–1,280, recorded HK$180–1,080 — check which before buying.** **Per-day dates now published (hkballet.com): live orchestra on 11–13, 18–20 and 22–26 Dec, both shows on Dec 24** — the Christmas Eve option *(verify the show times and that the one you buy is a live-music performance)*
- **The Cultural Centre's own calendar** lists everything in that building across the whole stay — the honest way to find what else is on *(performing-arts.gov.hk, venue code HKCC)*

## 🍜 Where to eat
- ⭐ **Yat Lok 一樂燒鵝**, G/F 34–38 Stanley St, Central — one Michelin star for roast goose, 10:00–20:30, **cash only**. ⚠️ **CLOSED WEDNESDAYS** (= Dec 23). Go 11:30 or 14:30, never 13:00
- ⭐ **Australia Dairy Company 澳洲牛奶公司**, 47 Parkes St, Jordan — 07:00–22:00, the scrambled eggs and the famously brisk service. ⚠️ **CLOSED THURSDAYS** (= Dec 24)
- ⭐ **Tim Ho Wan** — one Michelin star and still the cheapest starred food anywhere. The Sham Shui Po shop is the original
- ⭐ **Cheung Hing Kee** (Tsim Sha Tsui) — Bib Gourmand, Shanghai pan-fried soup buns 生煎包, crispy base and a thick doughy top
- **Din Tai Fung** (TST, Silvercord) — also Bib Gourmand; the safe, excellent, book-nothing option
- **Lung King Heen**, Four Seasons — the three-star splurge. **Minimum spend HK$700 + 10% per person from 1 Jan 2026**, and holiday lunches book out weeks ahead ☎ +852 3196 8880 *(verify the current terms when booking)*
- **Shek O village** — seafood straight off the Dragon's Back, bus 9 from Shau Kei Wan
- **Tai O** — dried seafood, charcoal egg waffles and tofu pudding in the stilt village; far better than eating on Lantau proper
- ⚠️ **Breakfast is NOT included at The Royal Garden** on any of the five mornings — budget them
- ⚠️ **Christmas festive menus and buffets sell out by early December.** Anything wanted on Dec 24–25 is a November booking, not a walk-in
