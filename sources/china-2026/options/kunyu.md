## ⭐ Our picks
- ⭐ **The park behind the school** — Kunyu Mountain National Forest Park is granite ridges, pine and old Taoist temple sites, and the trails start where the academy ends. The default rest-day outing, no transport needed *(verify the current gate fee and which trails are open in winter)*
- ⭐ **One full Yantai day** — Yantai Hill Park, the Suochengli lanes and a bowl of seafood noodles all sit within a walk of each other. Go once, do it properly, come back tired
- ⭐ **Penglai**, about an hour north — the sea-cliff pavilion and the old sea wall, near-empty in December. The one big sight worth leaving the school for
- ⭐ **A bathhouse or foot-massage afternoon** on a Sunday — cheap, hot, and exactly what a fortnight of training asks for. Ask the school where students go *(verify locally)*
- ⭐ **Sort the boring things in the first two days** — laundry, the village shop, how you get a taxi. It decides how comfortable the other twelve feel

## ➕ More options
- **Yangma Island** (养马岛) — a low coastal loop east of Muping, close to the academy; oyster shacks, empty beaches, hard wind
- **Zhifu Island** (芝罘岛) — the headland north of Yantai with a cliff path and a small fishing harbour; almost nobody in winter
- **Yantai Museum**, in the old Fujian Guild Hall — carved timber and sea-goddess halls, indoors and warm *(verify opening days)*
- **Weihai** for a day, about an hour by train — the seafront park, the Happiness Gate and the Liugong Island ferry *(verify winter ferry times before going)*
- **A hot spring** — the Kunyushan and Muping area has several resort spring pools; a plausible reward on a rest day *(verify which are open and how to reach them)*
- **A fish-market lunch in Yantai** — pick the catch, they cook it next door, you pay by weight *(verify locally; take cash)*
- Foggy or wet day: stay in and train forms in the hall. The ridge is exposed and the granite gets slick — this is the one place where the boring choice is the right one
- Evenings: the village has a shop and not much else. Download films, books and offline maps before you fly — there is nothing to buy locally and the VPN is not guaranteed

## 🍜 Where to eat
**The fortnight belongs to the temple — this is the only part of it the app plans.** Most meals come from the academy kitchen; that is what the monthly fee buys.
- ⭐ **Muping 牟平, ~30 minutes** — the nearest real town, where students go at weekends: a market street, supermarkets, malls. **Muping is known for its 烧烤 barbecue**, and that is the right reward after a training week
- ⭐ **三和园 Sanhe Yuan, Chaoyang Food Street, Yantai** — the city's signature **鲅鱼饺子 mackerel dumplings**. The one unmissable dish here
- ⭐ **福山大面 Fushan hand-pulled noodles** — a 200-year tradition with a seafood soup topping; Fushan is the birthplace of the style
- **Chaoyang Food Street** generally — Shandong seafood: sea cucumber with scallions, razor clams, prawns
- **烟台焖子 Yantai menzi** — fried bean jelly with shrimp paste, the local street snack
- **Penglai 小面** if the Penglai Pavilion day trip happens
- **Happiness Night Market** for cheap volume
- ⭐ **Yantai apples.** You arrive in apple season in the region famous for them — buy them by the bag
- The village around the school has a couple of good restaurants, and students rate the food near the academy well *(verify locally — this is student report, not a named address)*
