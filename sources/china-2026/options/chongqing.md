## ⭐ Our picks
- ⭐ **Liziba station** — the monorail that runs straight through an apartment block. The viewing platform below it is free and the whole thing takes ten minutes
- ⭐ **Ciqikou early**, before the tour buses — the back lanes and the river steps, not the souvenir street everyone photographs
- ⭐ **Kuixinglou** — the building whose "ground floor" is twenty-two storeys above the street on the other side. The purest 8D moment in the city, and it is free *(verify the walkway is open)*
- ⭐ **One split pot (鸳鸯锅) on the first night, ordered 微辣** — get the shock over with while you still have a second evening to enjoy
- ⭐ **The peninsula from Nanbin Road after dark** — the whole skyline in one frame from the far bank, and the crossing costs almost nothing

## ➕ More options
- **Raffles City sky bridge** at Chaotianmen — a paid deck over the confluence of the two rivers *(verify the ticket and the hours)*
- **Three Gorges Museum**, facing the People's Great Hall — free, warm and genuinely good on a fog day *(verify opening and whether booking is needed)*
- **Huguang Guild Hall** — restored Qing merchant halls and opera stages above the river, ten minutes from Jiefangbei
- **A two-rivers night cruise** from Chaotianmen — the lazy version of the skyline, about an hour *(verify winter departures)*
- **Nanshan and the One Tree viewing platform** — the high panorama, a taxi up and a taxi back
- **Sichuan Fine Arts Institute at Huangjueping** — a whole street of painted buildings; the odd, good, rainy-afternoon choice
- **Xiaomian for breakfast twice** — a different bowl at every stall, and it costs almost nothing
- Skip the day trips. Wulong and Fuling are both too far for two nights — the city itself has more in it than you will get through
