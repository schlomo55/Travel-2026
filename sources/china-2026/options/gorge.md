## ➕ More options
- **Jane's Guesthouse, Qiaotou** — holds the big bag while you hike (Option B, ≈¥5/day for non-guests); at the trailhead end, not the low-road end
- **Tina's Guesthouse** on the low road — the standard drop-off and pick-up point, where the shuttles gather, and the Dec 19 car's other stop
- **The staircase down to the middle rapids** — steep, about an hour, a separate small fee, and hard on the knees coming back up *(verify the current fee)*
- **The Sky Ladder at Walnut Garden** — the vertical ladder climb out of the gorge, for people who enjoy that sort of thing
- **The low road only**, by shuttle, if the weather turns — still spectacular, no exposure, no commitment
- **On to Shangri-La instead of back to Lijiang** — Qiaotou sits on that road too, if the plan ever changes *(verify buses in winter)*
- **Guesthouse dinner is a set meal at a fixed hour** — turn up for it or eat nothing *(verify the hour at check-in)*
- Rooms are unheated. Ask for the extra blanket before you need it, keep the headlamp reachable, and sleep in the hat if it comes to that
- The high trail is a single path with occasional red arrows — do not take shortcuts down towards the river, they are landslide chutes

## 🍜 Where to eat
- ⭐ **Tea Horse Guesthouse terrace, just past the 28 Bends** — the one warm meal between Qiaotou and bed, and the terrace *is* the view. Time the walk to land here at lunch
- ⭐ **Halfway Guesthouse's own kitchen** — well regarded, reasonably priced, and the upper balcony is the famous one. There is a coffee place on the main terrace
- **Tina's**, on the low road, for the meal at the end of day 2 while you wait for the Dali car
- ⚠️ **CASH ONLY, the whole trail. No ATMs.** Carry the ~¥1,500 already set aside for the gorge from the Nov 22 Beijing Daxing exchange (the cash plan, 28 Sep 2026) — nothing more to draw in Lijiang
- ⚠️ **Guesthouse dinner is a set meal at a fixed hour** — turn up for it or eat nothing *(verify the hour at check-in)*
- **Carry snacks and water for the walking hours.** Between the Bends and Halfway there is very little, and December afternoons are short
