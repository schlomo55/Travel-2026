### 1.1a THE REPLACEMENT — and it is a better Christmas Eve than the one it replaces
Researched 29 Jul 2026. **The substitute is not a compromise.**

**⭐ THE "IMMERSIVE LIGHT SHOW IN CENTRAL", the Hong Kong Tourism Board's WinterFest headline.**
Like-for-like and then some: free, outdoors, nightly, timed, light *and* music — except that
instead of lasers fired off rooftops it is **4K 3D projection mapping onto eight named landmark
buildings**, with over a dozen thematic scenes and holiday music. The eight are the **Bank of
China Building, Bank of China Tower, the Court of Final Appeal, Hong Kong City Hall High Block,
the Hong Kong Club Building, the HSBC Main Building, Prince's Building and the Standard
Chartered Bank Building** — the show is painted onto the actual architecture of colonial and
modern Central, not onto the sky. Thirty-odd trees and the pedestrian footbridges are lit with it.

**Why it fits this evening better than Symphony ever did:** it is in **Central**, so you get
there on the **Star Ferry** — a crossing already in the Dec 24 plan for its own sake. The evening
becomes one continuous thing: cross the harbour at dusk, walk the show, come back over the water.
Symphony asked you to stand still on a promenade for ten minutes; this asks you to walk through a
lit-up district for an hour.

**⭐ AIA CARNIVAL, Central Harbourfront** — five minutes from the light show, and it opens for the
season **around 22 Dec** (the 2025–26 edition ran 22 Dec – 1 Mar at the Central Harbourfront Event
Space, 9 Lung Wo Road). Ferris wheel over the harbour, rides, games, food stalls. It turns the
Central evening from one show into a night out. <span>verify</span> the 2026–27 dates and the
admission model.

**◆ AQUA LUNA, the red-sail junk** — 45-minute evening cruise on Victoria Harbour, a drink
included, bookable on **Klook**. The paid option, and the one that most directly replaces what
Symphony was *for*: seeing the harbour lit up. It stands on its own with no show running, which
is exactly why it is on this list.

**◆ Drone shows.** Hong Kong flew large drone displays over Victoria Harbour repeatedly through
2026 — Lunar New Year, National Security Education Day, and 2,026 drones for the 29th anniversary
of the handover. None is scheduled for Christmas, but it is the format the city is visibly moving
to, so it is worth asking.

**◆ The government's own named replacement is still unscheduled.** Tourism minister Rosanna Law
announced a **new light-festival-themed show for the second half of 2026**, with the Tourism Board
"exploring" **Victoria Peak** as a venue. That is a plan, not a listing with times — so The Peak
stays in the Dec 24 *morning*, where it belongs, and is not counted on for the evening.
