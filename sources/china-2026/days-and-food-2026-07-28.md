### 1.1a THE REPLACEMENT — and it is a better Christmas Eve than the one it replaces
Researched 29 Jul 2026. **The substitute is not a compromise.**

**⭐ THE "IMMERSIVE LIGHT SHOW IN CENTRAL", the Hong Kong Tourism Board's WinterFest headline.**
Like-for-like and then some: free, outdoors, nightly, timed, light *and* music — except that
instead of lasers fired off rooftops it is **4K 3D projection mapping onto eight named landmark
buildings**, with over a dozen thematic scenes and holiday music. The eight are the **Bank of
China Building, Bank of China Tower, the Court of Final Appeal, Hong Kong City Hall High Block,
the Hong Kong Club Building, the HSBC Main Building, Prince's Building and the Standard
Chartered Bank Building** — the show is painted onto the actual architecture of colonial and
modern Central, not onto the sky. Thirty-odd trees and the pedestrian footbridges are lit with it.

**Why it fits this evening better than Symphony ever did:** it is in **Central**, so you get
there on the **Star Ferry** — a crossing already in the Dec 24 plan for its own sake. The evening
becomes one continuous thing: cross the harbour at dusk, walk the show, come back over the water.
Symphony asked you to stand still on a promenade for ten minutes; this asks you to walk through a
lit-up district for an hour.

**⭐ AIA CARNIVAL, Central Harbourfront** — five minutes from the light show, and it opens for the
season **around 22 Dec** (the 2025–26 edition ran 22 Dec – 1 Mar at the Central Harbourfront Event
Space, 9 Lung Wo Road). Ferris wheel over the harbour, rides, games, food stalls. It turns the
Central evening from one show into a night out. <span>verify</span> the 2026–27 dates and the
admission model.

**◆ AQUA LUNA, the red-sail junk** — 45-minute evening cruise on Victoria Harbour, a drink
included, bookable on **Klook**. The paid option, and the one that most directly replaces what
Symphony was *for*: seeing the harbour lit up. It stands on its own with no show running, which
is exactly why it is on this list.

**◆ Drone shows.** Hong Kong flew large drone displays over Victoria Harbour repeatedly through
2026 — Lunar New Year, National Security Education Day, and 2,026 drones for the 29th anniversary
of the handover. None is scheduled for Christmas, but it is the format the city is visibly moving
to, so it is worth asking.

**◆ The government's own named replacement is still unscheduled.** Tourism minister Rosanna Law
announced a **new light-festival-themed show for the second half of 2026**, with the Tourism Board
"exploring" **Victoria Peak** as a venue. That is a plan, not a listing with times — so The Peak
stays in the Dec 24 *morning*, where it belongs, and is not counted on for the evening.

## 3. DAY BY DAY

Each day: **the anchor**, **food** (⭐ = the pick), **spare** (droppable), **⏰** (anything
time-bound). Beds are as booked and are not restated.

### Dec 5 · Sat — Academy → Beijing · *Four Points Sanlitun*
- **Anchor:** the Sanlitun night the plan has always protected — Taikoo Li, bar street, then
  the midnight massage at **曲水兰亭 Qushui Lanting** (leave the bars ~23:15; fallback **菩提
  Bodhi**, 5 min walk).
- ⭐ **Food — 四季民福 Siji Minfu**, the Peking-duck house locals actually name. Whole duck
  ¥168–228, no tourist surcharge. There is a branch near Taikoo Li <span>verify</span>; the
  famous one is at **Donghuamen, by the Forbidden City's east gate** — save that one for
  tomorrow, it is the better lunch than dinner.
- **Alternative if you want modern Beijing instead:** Taikoo Li's own upper-floor restaurants.
  You are staying 1.2 km away and it is the one night the plan calls for a late one.
- **Spare:** nothing. Travel day + a late night is already a full day.
- ⏰ **At check-in, ask the Four Points desk to confirm late spa entry and book Qushui Lanting.**

### Dec 6 · Sun — Forbidden City · *Four Points Sanlitun*
- **Anchor:** **Forbidden City** — passport IS the ticket, ¥40, booked Nov 29. Then **Jingshan
  Park** directly north for the sunset roofline, and **Beihai Park**'s White Dagoba ten minutes
  further on if the light holds.
- ⭐ **Food — 四季民福 Siji Minfu, Donghuamen branch**, 5 minutes from the palace's east exit.
  Ask for a window table: it looks straight at the moat and the corner tower. Book ahead —
  weekend waits run 90 minutes.
- **Evening:** back to Sanlitun; **Bodhi tuina + cupping**, ~¥288/90 min.
- **Spare (grey/freezing day):** the Palace Museum's own indoor galleries — ceramics, clocks,
  the treasure halls — are included in the ticket and warm.
- ⏰ **Book the Dali→Kunming train today** (visa-critical, 15-day window opens).
  ⏰ **Book the Zhangjiajie Forest Park ticket tonight** — see §1.2.

### Dec 7 · Mon — Jinshanling, the wild wall · *Blossom House, Houhai*
- **Anchor:** **06:30 pickup at Four Points** (check out first — the pack rides in the driver's
  trunk). In-gate → **East Five-Window Tower** ("Tower 22"), ~7 km, 3.5–5 h. Turning down by
  ~13:30, out the East Gate before 15:00. Driver drops you and the bag at **Blossom House
  ~17:30**.
- ⭐ **Food:** **carry it.** There is no reliable food on that stretch of wall in December and
  no signal to summon any. Buy at Sanlitun the night before — bread, nuts, chocolate, and
  **two litres of water minimum**. The driver will stop for a roadside lunch on request; take
  it if offered, it is the warm meal of the day.
- **Evening — deep tuina near Gulou, ~¥220**, then early sleep. Legs matter on Dec 9.
- ⭐ **If you can still face a meal: 姚记炒肝 Yaoji Chaoguan, 311 Gulou Dongdajie** — one of the
  last places serving the real Beijing stew, plus baozi and **炸酱面 zhajiangmian**. Cheap, hot,
  five minutes from the bed. <span>verify</span> its evening hours; it is a breakfast institution
  first.
- ⚠️ **No signal all day.** Nothing that needs booking may be scheduled for today.

### Dec 8 · Tue — Spa day, and the trip's decision day · *Blossom House, Houhai*
- **Anchor:** clinical morning at **护国寺中医医院 骨伤科** bone-setting — 航空胡同42号,
  ☎ 56895700, ~¥150–600 <span>verify</span>. Then **all-day hot springs at Jiuhua Resort,

- ⭐ **Food — 南门涮肉 Nanmen Hotpot**, Beijing's copper-pot instant-boiled mutton, charcoal

  meal and today is the day for it. The original is by the **south gate of the Temple of
  Heaven** — a long way from Houhai, so <span>verify</span> a nearer branch, or accept the
  taxi. Equivalent classics: **东来顺 Donglaishun**, **一条龙 Yitiaolong**.
- **Spare:** the **National Museum** on Tiananmen Square — free, enormous, warm, and the right
  answer if the day turns filthy. Reserve at 17:00 on Dec 5 for a Dec 8 slot.
- ⏰⏰ **THE BIG DAY FOR MONEY.** Element's free-cancel window shuts at **00:00 tonight**

  **auto-charged today**. Pullman's own free window shuts **23:59 tomorrow**.
  ⏰ Zhangjiajie park ticket, if not done Dec 6.

## 5. FOOD NEAR THE ACADEMY (Nov 22 – Dec 5)
**The fortnight itself is the temple's.** This section exists only so that when the academy
releases you, you know where to walk.

  market street, big supermarkets, malls. ⭐ **Muping is known for its barbecue 烧烤** — that is
  the local thing to eat, and it is the right reward after a training week.
- **Yantai city, for a Sunday.**
  - ⭐ **三和园 Sanhe Yuan, Chaoyang Food Street** — Yantai's signature **鲅鱼饺子 mackerel
    dumplings**. The city's one unmissable dish.
  - ⭐ **福山大面 Fushan hand-pulled noodles** — a 200-year noodle tradition, seafood soup
    topping. Fushan is the birthplace of the noodle style.
  - **Chaoyang Food Street** generally for Shandong seafood: sea cucumber with scallions,
    razor clams, prawns.
  - **烟台焖子 Yantai menzi** — fried bean jelly with shrimp paste, the street snack.
  - **Penglai 小面 noodles**, if the **Penglai Pavilion** day trip happens: sea-cliff tower
    from 1061, near-empty in December.
  - **Happiness Night Market** for cheap volume.
- **Yantai fruit.** You arrive in apple season and Yantai apples are the best in China. Buy
  them by the bag.
