Two weeks at the academy is the longest stay of the whole trip, and the rhythm does the work for you: train, eat, sleep, repeat. The mountains around Kunyushan are a national park of granite ridges and Taoist temple sites — on rest days the trails right behind the school are usually the best thing available, no transport needed.

Late November on the Shandong coast is properly cold and dry — often clear blue days around or below freezing. Training clothes that layer beat one heavy jacket: a base layer, a fleece, and a windproof shell cover everything from morning forms outside to evening classes indoors. Bring more socks than feels reasonable.

Yantai city is reachable for a day out (about an hour by car — *verify the school's usual arrangement*); most students group up and share a driver. **Cash matters here more than anywhere else on the trip:** the school itself asks for cash (it takes USD, EUR or RMB; the owner pays USD), there is no ATM near the school and the nearest town is 30 minutes away — so, per the cash plan (28 Sep 2026), no ATM is used at all: the RMB this village needs comes from changing the Exchange envelope (US$600 → ≈¥4,200) at the Beijing Daxing arrivals counter on the way in.
