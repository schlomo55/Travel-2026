Tiger Leaping Gorge is one of the deepest gorges on earth — the Jinsha (upper Yangtze) thundering between Jade Dragon and Haba Snow Mountains. December is the **dry season: stable trails, clear views**, cold mornings and pleasant walking by mid-day.

With one night, the classic move is the **high trail** as far as the guesthouses (Naxi Family, Tea Horse, Halfway) rather than racing the whole traverse: start early, take the 28 Bends slowly — it's the only genuinely hard hour — and let the gorge do the rest. The views from the Halfway terraces at sunset justify the entire detour. The low road and the riverside Tiger Leaping Stone are a separate short visit, reachable by shuttle from the trailhead towns *(verify times locally — they thin out in winter)*.

Real shoes, water, sun cream (the UV at this altitude is stronger than the temperature suggests), and a headlamp just in case. Guesthouses stay open in December but evenings are cold — ask for the extra blanket before you need it.
