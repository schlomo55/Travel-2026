Chongli is China's Olympic ski country — high, cold and reliably snowy from late November. For a first time on skis, book a **lesson for the first morning** rather than the second day: one hour of basics saves two days of frustration. English-speaking instructors exist at the bigger resorts but are limited — reserve ahead *(verify availability when booking)*.

Rental covers skis, boots and helmet; it usually does **not** include goggles, gloves or a face covering, and at −15°C on a lift all three are non-negotiable. Buy hand warmers at any convenience store and put one in each boot at lunch.

Pace the first day: the beginner zones are wide and forgiving, and the magic-carpet lifts mean no chairlift stress until you want it. Drink more water than you think you need — the air is extremely dry — and stop while your legs still feel good; almost every first-day injury happens on the "one last run".
