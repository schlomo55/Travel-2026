The sandstone pillars of Wulingyuan are what *Avatar* borrowed, and December is the quiet season: thin crowds, low prices, and fog that either steals the view or makes it — often both in one day. Build the days flexible: if the morning is socked in, do the lower valley (Golden Whip Stream) and save the viewpoints for the first clearing.

The park ticket covers multiple days *(verify the current validity when buying)*; inside, the **Bailong glass elevator** and the cable cars are the knees-saving way up — queues that are brutal in summer are short in December, but wind can pause them, so never plan the last descent tight. Tianzi Mountain and Yuanjiajie hold the classic pillar panoramas.

Winter practicalities: the stone stairs stay damp and slick under fog — shoes with real grip matter more than warmth. Temperatures hover around 0–8°C; the valley floor is milder than the tops. Monkeys near the stream are charming and will unzip your bag — keep food buried.
