Kunming is the safety night before the exit flight — but it hides a genuinely wonderful December ritual: every winter, thousands of **red-billed gulls from Siberia** descend on the city, and **Green Lake (Cuihu)** turns into a swirling white spectacle. They arrive in November and stay through the winter, so your date lands exactly in the window. An hour at the lake at golden hour, a bag of bread you probably shouldn't buy but will, and the trip gets one last unplanned highlight.

Around the lake, Wenlin Street and the university quarter do relaxed cafés and Yunnan coffee — the province grows most of China's beans, and it's very good.

Keep the evening honest: this night exists to make the visa deadline unmissable. Pack for the morning before going out, confirm the airport transfer time, and treat everything else as bonus.
