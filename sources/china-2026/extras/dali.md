Dali is the exhale after the gorge: a walled old town between the **Cangshan range** and **Erhai lake**, at a gentler 1,900 m. In December the light is sharp and golden and the famous Xiaguan wind is real — sunny afternoons, cold gusts, perfect walking weather in layers.

With one night, keep it simple: the **Three Pagodas** with the mountains behind them are the signature view (the reflection pond at the back of the complex is the photo), then the old town's gates and the food street as the lanterns come on. If there's a spare hour, any stretch of the Erhai lakeside path delivers — wintering birds included.

Dali baba (stuffed flatbread) and a pot of local pu'er in a courtyard teahouse cover lunch properly. Rose-petal pastries are everywhere and travel well as small gifts.
