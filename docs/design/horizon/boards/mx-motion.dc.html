Round 3 · the Map, explored

Route motion — four styles

The same route — Beijing → Chongli → Zhangjiajie — animated four ways. Every style stops when Calm motion is on.

01

Flow

On the map now

Dashes stream along the route, all the time — calm and always alive. This is what the map does now.

02

Comet

One bright point flies the route and leaves a fading tail — you see the direction and the pace at a glance.

03

Ink

The route paints itself like a brush stroke, then gets a seal. A nod to Chinese ink painting.

04

Relay

Each stop lights up, then passes the light to the next leg — the order of the trip, told one step at a time.
